Top-Tier Tactical Play Patterns

When deploying **6-letter words starting with A**, there are three critical rules to keep in mind to keep your win-streak intact:

  • **Avoid Hard Mode Deadlocks**: In daily puzzle games, starting with a word that begins with A can be incredibly powerful, but watch out for similar sounding structures (like **A-*-*-*-*-E**). Sacrificing a move to test multiple consonants is often much safer than guessing sequentially.
  • **Leverage Y Hooking**: Six-letter words ending in Y, such as **AGENCY**, allow you to play off existing tiles horizontally without cluttering your rack.
  • **Target High-Point Tiles**: Capitalize on X, K, and V early before your opponent can close out the board's bonus multipliers.
